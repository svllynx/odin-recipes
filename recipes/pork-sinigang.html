Pork Sinigang

Description

Sinigang is a sour Filipino soup. The sour taste comes from tamarind. We cook pork with tomatoes, onion and vegetables. It is very good to eat when it is raining.

Ingredients

  • 1 kg pork belly or ribs
  • 2 liters water
  • 1 pack tamarind soup mix
  • 2 tomatoes
  • 1 onion
  • 1 radish
  • 1 bundle string beans
  • 2 eggplants
  • 1 bundle kangkong
  • 2 green chili
  • Fish sauce

Steps

  1. Boil the pork in water. Remove the dirty foam on top.
  2. Add the onion and tomatoes. Cook for about 45 minutes until the pork is soft.
  3. Add the tamarind soup mix.
  4. Add the radish and cook for 5 minutes.
  5. Add the string beans, eggplant and chili. Cook for 5 more minutes.
  6. Add fish sauce until it tastes good to you.
  7. Turn off the fire. Add the kangkong and cover the pot for 2 minutes. Serve it hot.
