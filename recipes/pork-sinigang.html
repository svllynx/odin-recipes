Pork Sinigang

Description

Sinigang is a sour and comforting Filipino soup soured with tamarind. Tender pork is simmered with tomatoes, onions and a mix of vegetables like radish, string beans and kangkong. It is perfect for rainy days.

Ingredients

  • 1 kg pork belly or ribs, cut into pieces
  • 2 liters water
  • 1 packet (40 g) tamarind soup mix, or 200 g fresh tamarind
  • 2 tomatoes, quartered
  • 1 onion, quartered
  • 1 radish (labanos), sliced
  • 1 bundle string beans (sitaw), cut
  • 2 eggplants, sliced
  • 1 bundle kangkong (water spinach)
  • 2 long green chilies
  • Fish sauce (patis) to taste

Steps

  1. Boil the pork in water, skimming off the scum that rises to the top.
  2. Add the onion and tomatoes. Simmer for about 45 minutes until the pork is tender.
  3. Stir in the tamarind soup mix (or strained fresh tamarind juice).
  4. Add the radish and cook for 5 minutes.
  5. Add the string beans, eggplant and chilies. Cook for another 5 minutes.
  6. Season with fish sauce to taste.
  7. Turn off the heat, add the kangkong and cover for 2 minutes. Serve hot.
